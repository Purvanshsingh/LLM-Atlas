02 · Inside the model

Follow your words through the network.

This is your model, live. Every coloured square is a real number inside it right now, and they shift as it trains. Hover over a row to follow one letter, click any stage to open it up, or let the walkthrough reveal it step by step.

Where learning happens

The backward pass, live

After every guess, the error flows backwards from the output to the embeddings. Each meter shows how hard that part's weights are being pushed right now (the size of its gradient). Watch the meters settle as the loss falls.

04 · What letters mean to it

Letters find their neighbours.

The model stores every character as a list of 32 numbers, its embedding. Squash those 32 dimensions down to two and you get this map. It starts as random scatter. As training goes on, characters that behave alike tend to drift together.

The story behind LLM Atlas

From reading to understanding.

After working through Sebastian Raschka’s Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch), I wanted to turn what I’d learned into something you could see, touch, and experiment with.

LLM Atlas is my attempt to make the journey from tokens to transformers more intuitive, without needing to read hundreds of pages before getting started.
